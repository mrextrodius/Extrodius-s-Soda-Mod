## Page text (paste into CurseForge description)

# Extrodius's Soda Mod

Use new machines to brew 9 different soda cans, each with their own benefits.
Better than a base potion, never better than an extended one. One of them is
brewed in secret — the mixer isn't telling how.

## How it works

1. **Soda Mixer** — Dye + sugar + an ingredient + an empty soda can gives you a
   flat soda. Click to mix; the order doesn't matter.
2. **Gasifier** — Wheat + fuel + an empty CO2 canister gives you a full canister.
   It glows while it's working.
3. **Carbonator** — A full canister + a flat soda gives you a drinkable soda.
   The empty canister comes back — it's a deposit loop.

**Getting started:** craft a **Soda Can** (6 iron nuggets, 2-wide column — left
or right side) and an empty **CO2 Canister** (iron nugget + iron nugget + copper
nugget, any horizontal row). Every recipe is obtainable in the Overworld —
no Nether or End required.

## The 9 sodas

| Soda | Mixer recipe (dye + ingredient) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Cocoa Cola | Black dye + cocoa beans | Haste I (4:00) |
| Dr. Blaze | Red dye + sweet berries | Fire Resistance (4:00) |
| Phanta Slime | Lime dye + melon slice | Jump Boost I (4:00) |
| Mountain Creeper | Yellow dye + glow berries | Speed I (4:00) |
| Phanta Orange | Orange dye + pumpkin | Absorption II (2:00) |
| Spruce Beer | Brown dye + spruce sapling | Resistance I (4:00) |
| Phanta Cream | White dye + milk bucket (empty bucket returned) | Regeneration I (1:00) |
| Phanta Grape | Purple dye + lilac | Night Vision (4:00) |
| Graveyard | Secret (see ??? below) | All 8 effects at once + Undying (4:00) |

Every mixer recipe also needs **sugar** and an **empty soda can**.

## The catch: caffeine crash

When a soda's effect wears off, you get a mild crash — Nausea (0:01) + Slowness
I (0:05). It won't trigger if you still have the effect from another source.
Charming, not punishing. The Graveyard's crash hits harder — Nausea (0:03) +
Slowness III (0:10) when its longest effect wears off.
